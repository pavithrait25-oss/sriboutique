HERITAGE & CRAFTSMANSHIP

Welcome to Sri Boutique

Rooted in the rich cultural soil of Hosur, Tamil Nadu, Sri Boutique was born from a timeless love for authentic Indian handlooms and the regal splendour of Indian sarees.

At Sri Boutique, we believe a saree is never just six yards of fabric—it is an heirloom of sacred celebrations, familial warmth, and effortless grace. Every saree in our boutique is individually curated directly from legendary master weavers in Kanchipuram, Arani, Salem, and Coimbatore.

Whether you are walking down the aisle in a resplendent bridal Kanchipuram Silk, gracing a family festive gathering in a lightweight Soft Silk, seeking everyday artisanal comfort in breathable Handloom Cotton, or making heads turn in an exquisite Designer Saree, Sri Boutique wraps you in warmth, purity, and unparalleled tradition.
