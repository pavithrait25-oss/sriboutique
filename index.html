Our Heritage & Craftsmanship

Sri Boutique, Hosur, Tamil Nadu

Nestled in the vibrant town of Hosur, Tamil Nadu, India, Sri Boutique was born from a deep reverence for India’s glorious textile heritage. We bridge the gap between traditional handloom weaver communities and modern women who seek authenticity without astronomical showroom markups.

From the sacred looms of Kanchipuram and Varanasi to the earthy handlooms of Chettinad, each saree in our collection is personally inspected for pure zari luster, fabric density, and fall. Whether you are adorning a bride for her sacred Muhurtham or selecting a breezy cotton saree for daily grace, Sri Boutique treats every patron like family.
